01

TEVEO

UX/UI Designer at Small Giants · 2025–2026

Problem

The old Shopify site was outdated. It was built for a niche leggings brand, but TEVEO had grown into a €50M+ unisex sportswear company. With 24+ drops a year, this caused real issues: a confusing shopping experience, low customer trust, and no way to measure what worked.

My Role

I took ownership after a senior designer defined the initial visual direction, translating those concepts into a scalable, reusable component system while focusing on CRO improvements and a smooth handoff to development.

Key contributions

  • Built 40+ components and page templates for a scalable foundation
  • Redesigned navigation to scale with a wider unisex catalog without hurting findability
  • Redesigned Homepage, PLP, PDP, and About Us page with scannable layouts and stronger trust signals to support conversion
  • Collaborated with the PM, CRO, developers, and TEVEO's marketing team to align on priorities

04

Echo Device
Setup

Amazon · 2019 – 2020

Millions of Echo smart speaker customers use the Alexa App to set up their devices, but almost half did not complete setup on their first try. With a jarring and inconsistent experience, customer confidence was lacking throughout the entire flow.

To combat this, I rebuilt our foundations to deliver systematic design improvements for all Echo smart speaker setup flows. This led to dramatic increases in setup completion rates, setup app ratings, CX consistency, and time savings in design and engineering.

+213% Increased setup app rating
+11% Increased setup completion rate
−65% Reduced design & engineering time
05

Echo Show
Security

Amazon · 2020

Guard is an Alexa service that helps monitor customers' homes while they're away. Using the Echo devices as a pair of ears, it can listen for sounds of dangerous activity and notify customers. However, the Guard experience was limited to voice and mobile.

I designed concepts to bring the Guard experience to the Echo Show. I led the end-to-end design of the v1 experience, enabling customers to use their Echo Shows as a security panel for their Guard Plus, Ring, or other 3P security system. The final experience is a unique combination of visuals, sound, and motion coming together in harmony.

06

Alexa Fitness

Amazon · 2020

Echo Buds is a device that allows customers to access Alexa on-the-go. Our vision was to unlock a key use case for the Echo Buds customer—fitness—as a way to drive engagement for the on-the-go customer segment.

My team and I designed and shipped the end-to-end mobile app experience that allows Echo Bud customers to track their runs, walks, and other workouts via duration, steps, distance, pace, calories, and route.
