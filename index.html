01

TEVEO

Small Giants · E-commerce · 2025 - 2026

Problem

TEVEO's Shopify site was originally built for a niche leggings brand, but they had grown into a €50M+ unisex sportswear company. With 24+ product drops a year, this caused real issues: the website became harder to navigate, customers struggled to find products, and the team had no insights into what was driving conversions.

My Role

I took ownership after a lead designer defined the visual direction, translating those concepts into a scalable design system, while focusing on CRO improvements and a smooth handoff to development.

Key contributions

  • Designed a library of 40+ reusable components and page templates to create a scalable foundation for future growth.
  • Redesigned the navigation to support TEVEO's expanding unisex product range while keeping products easy to discover.
  • Redesigned key pages (Homepage, PLP, PDP, and About Us page) to improve readability, highlight trust signals, and create a clearer path to purchase.
  • Worked closely with the Product Manager, CRO, Dev team, and TEVEO's marketing team to align on business goals, technical constraints, and user needs.

02

Verstegen

Small Giants · Website · Design · 2025

Problem

Verstegen's WooCommerce website no longer matched the needs of the business. Products were difficult to find, product pages lacked the information customers needed, and frequent checkout issues hurt the buying experience. Because every update depended on an external agency, the team couldn't improve the site independently. B2B customers were sent to a separate website, creating an inconsistent brand experience, while limited analytics and poor accessibility made it difficult to optimise the experience over time.

My Role

As the UX/UI designer, I worked closely with the client lead, CRO specialist, and frontend developers throughout the project. We started with a client meeting to define the requirements for the new website. I redesigned the entire website, transforming the existing catalogue style website into a more user-friendly e-commerce Shopify platform.

Key contributions

  • Ran user interviews and card sorting to define how people expect products to be grouped, then redesign the category structure and navigation around that
  • Removed the old "Professionals" redirect, bringing B2C and B2B into one shopping experience
  • Worked with CRO to set a clear goal for each page type, so pages were designed with a purpose
  • Optimised product pages with clearer USPs, trust signals, and reviews, and adding expandable sections for preparation, ingredients, allergens, and nutritional information to help customers quickly find the details they need.
  • Improved product discovery, optimised pages, and a more accessible and consistent visual design
  • Designed to meet basic WCAG accessibility requirements
  • Handed off a fully specced Figma file with all assets ready for development

05

Echo Show
Security

Amazon · 2020

Guard is an Alexa service that helps monitor customers' homes while they're away. Using the Echo devices as a pair of ears, it can listen for sounds of dangerous activity and notify customers. However, the Guard experience was limited to voice and mobile.

I designed concepts to bring the Guard experience to the Echo Show. I led the end-to-end design of the v1 experience, enabling customers to use their Echo Shows as a security panel for their Guard Plus, Ring, or other 3P security system. The final experience is a unique combination of visuals, sound, and motion coming together in harmony.

Echo Device
Setup

Amazon · 2019 – 2020

Millions of Echo smart speaker customers use the Alexa App to set up their devices, but almost half did not complete setup on their first try. With a jarring and inconsistent experience, customer confidence was lacking throughout the entire flow.

To combat this, I rebuilt our foundations to deliver systematic design improvements for all Echo smart speaker setup flows. This led to dramatic increases in setup completion rates, setup app ratings, CX consistency, and time savings in design and engineering.
